How we calculate

Consumables are charged per henchman, per day:

  • Ration packs: 3
  • Water bottles (1 L): 4
  • Energy tonic cans: 2 (3 with night-shift crew)
  • Throat lozenges for villain monologues: 1

Lair power cells are a flat rate per day, regardless of crew size: Volcano 5, Arctic 8, Orbital 12.

  • Safety buffer (0%, 10% or 20%) is added to consumables only. Results are rounded up.
  • Hazard suits: 1 per henchman for the whole rental, no buffer.
